\section{Societal Aspects}

Surveillance compression research inherits the societal footprint of surveillance itself: efficiency improvements can serve public safety, traffic management, and industrial monitoring \cite{Shao2018Smart,Chen2019DIVS}, but they can also lower the cost barrier to intrusive observation. The responsible framing, adopted here, is that technical work must be paired with governance---retention limits, access controls, and audit mechanisms such as the tamper-evident manifests of Chapter~6, whose scope is honestly bounded \cite{Kanwal2020Chain}.

\section{Health Aspects}

The project involved no human subjects research, no wearable or biometric data collection beyond standard camera footage recorded by the team, and no health intervention. Operator-facing health considerations (monitoring fatigue, effects of spatially varying quality on sustained attention) are out of scope and recorded as future human-factors work.

\section{Safety Aspects}

The system performs no actuation and issues no safety-critical commands; PTZ and audio modules present in the repository were not part of the evaluated path. A misbehaving ROI allocator degrades footage quality but cannot directly cause physical harm; the safety-relevant risk is evidentiary (missing exculpatory detail), addressed through retention policy and integrity design rather than through any claim made here.

\section{Legal Aspects}

As stated in Chapters~7 and 10: exact-byte hash-chain verification is not legal admissibility, certification, or standards compliance. Any evidentiary use would require jurisdictional procedures, qualified custody controls, and expert testimony beyond the scope of this undergraduate project.

\section{Cultural Aspects}

Acceptable camera coverage, retention periods, and privacy configurations differ across institutions and cultures. The system's YAML profiles and privacy modes are the technical hooks for such adaptation; deciding correct settings for any real deployment is a matter for the deploying institution, not for this thesis.
